📋 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect minimal information to run MyLibrary and connect books with readers.

A. Information You Provide to Us

When you use MyLibrary, you may provide:

  • Name – so we know who you are and who has our books
  • Email address – to create your account and send notifications
  • Phone number optional – for text reminders if you prefer
  • Mailing address only if needed – if we need to ship books to you
  • Password – to secure your account
  • Book reviews/ratings optional – if you choose to leave them

B. Information We Automatically Collect

When you visit our website, we may collect:

  • IP address – for basic security and to prevent abuse
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages you visit on our site
  • Time and date of your visit
  • Time spent on pages

C. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use basic cookies to:

  • Keep you logged in to your account
  • Remember your preferences
  • Understand how people use our site (basic analytics)

⚠️ Note: You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some website features may not work properly.

🔍 2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information only for:

📚 Library Operations

  • Creating and managing your membership account
  • Tracking which books you've borrowed
  • Sending due date reminders
  • Notifying you when requested books are available
  • Contacting you about overdue or lost books

📈 Website Improvement

  • Understanding how people use the website
  • Fixing technical issues
  • Improving the browsing experience

💬 Communication

  • Responding to your questions or messages
  • Sending important account updates not marketing

🤝 3. INFORMATION SHARING – OUR PROMISE TO YOU

❌ I Will NEVER:

  • Sell your information to anyone – ever
  • Share your information with third parties for marketing
  • Rent your email address to spammers
  • Post your borrowing history publicly

⚠️ I MAY Share Information Only In These Limited Situations:

  • With your consent – if you ask me to share something specific
  • Legal requirements – if required by law, court order, or legal process
  • Protection of rights – to protect the safety of my books, myself, or other members

🔒 4. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

🔐

Your information is stored on secure servers with password protection

🔒

All data transmitted between your browser and our website is encrypted (HTTPS)

⚡

Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text

🔄

I regularly update security practices to protect your information

However: No online transmission is 100% secure. While I work hard to protect your data, I cannot guarantee absolute security.

⏳ 5. DATA RETENTION

Account Type Retention Period
Active accounts Information kept while your membership is active
Inactive accounts If you haven't borrowed books fo 1 year, I may delete your information
Closed accounts When you close your account, I will delete your personal information within 30 days, except:
  • Records of lost or unreturned books (until resolved)
  • Information I'm required to keep by law

⚖️ 6. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

You have the right to:

👁️

Access

Request a copy of the information I have about you

✏️

Correct

Update or fix inaccurate information (you can do this in your account settings)

🗑️

Delete

Request deletion of your account and information

📧

Opt-out

Unsubscribe from emails (though you'll still receive important account notices)

To exercise these rights, contact me at [your email] or use your account settings.

👶 7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

MyLibrary is intended for users aged 13 or more than it, I do not knowingly collect information from children under this age. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact me immediately.

🔗 8. THIRD-PARTY LINKS

Our website may link to:

  • Book seller sites (like Amazon or Bookshop.org)
  • Goodreads or other book communities
  • Social media

I am not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites. Please review their policies.

📝 9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If I make changes to this Privacy Policy, I'll post them here with an updated "Effective Date." For significant changes, I'll notify members by email.
